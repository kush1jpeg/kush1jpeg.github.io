avant-garde, a peculiar word i came across, long ago, while reading beyond-good and evil by Friedrich Nietzsche(yes the guy with the big mustache),
i wrote a post back then, but decided to research a little further on it today, it may not feel very structured cuz its just a collection of thoughts and learnings;

i got to know its a term widely used in fashion industry, cinema, literatur3, very new to a sprouting-youth like me- can be used to depict an idea very much opposite to the current beliefs, to the point its considered idiotic: something  contemporary, new, a logical end to our great values/thinking/identity;  marks the end of something old being replaced by something new/radical, the transitioning phase where the old doesnt exactly accept the change is avant-garde; literally translates as “before, or advance-guard”. Something after the time period would be termed as arrier grande, people in the creative field tend to be referred by this word cuz they carry the burden of creation - its an obligation of artists to "serve as the avant-garde of the people";

Now coming to the whole topic of discussion- from the book;
the exact phrase which i vaguely remember was around the idea-
> A woman painting with menstrual blood and calling it patriarchal decay

lemme accept, this statement itself deserves a great philosophical discussion, afterall Nietzsche said such a great phrase just like that: accords to me, its him being satirical, about the fact that the very thing, the menstrual blood, which makes up the biological identity of a women will act as a form of confrontation to the patriarchal society. the very sign of fertility, disgust(in many regions) will prove as a weapon against the oppressors.
